# Personas — HeartRateCoach

## Persona: Alex — Active Fitness Enthusiast

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Alex |
| **Age** | 32 |
| **Occupation** | Mid-level professional (office job, sedentary during work hours) |
| **Location** | Urban / suburban — runs outdoors and on treadmill |
| **Devices** | iPhone 14+, Apple Watch Series 8+ |

---

### Goals

- Train smarter, not just harder — stop guessing effort level by feel
- Improve aerobic base and cardiovascular health over 3–6 months
- Get real-time guidance during runs without looking at a phone
- Understand and apply heart rate zone training in a structured way
- Track progress over time and see improvement in time-in-zone data

---

### Frustrations

- Generic workout apps use population-average heart rate targets that don't fit individual physiology
- Existing apps require too much manual setup or are too complex to use mid-run
- No immediate feedback when effort is too high or too low — only sees data after the workout
- Loses motivation when workouts feel unstructured or without a clear target

---

### Behaviours & Habits

- Runs 3–4 times per week, varying between steady-state runs and interval sessions
- Always wears Apple Watch during workouts — rarely carries phone in hand
- Uses AirPods during runs — comfortable receiving audio coaching
- Checks Apple Health occasionally but doesn't analyse detailed metrics
- Prefers workouts that are guided and time-bounded (knows a session will be 35 minutes)

---

### Technical Comfort

- Comfortable with iPhone and standard iOS apps
- Familiar with Apple Health concepts (step count, heart rate, calories)
- Not a cardiologist — understands "zones" at a surface level but needs the app to do the math
- Comfortable with Sign in with Apple

---

### Relationship to the App

Alex opens the app, sets a goal (fat burn or endurance), picks a workout type, and runs. The app tells Alex exactly when to push harder and when to back off — based on Alex's own resting heart rate and age, not a chart from the internet. After the workout, Alex reviews time-in-zones to understand how the session went.

---

> **Representative quote:**
> *"I want the app to tell me exactly when to speed up or slow down — based on MY heart rate, not a number someone averaged across thousands of strangers."*

---

## Story Mapping

All user stories in `stories.md` are written from Alex's perspective. The single persona approach reflects that HeartRateCoach v1 delivers one unified experience to all users — personalization is achieved through the Karvonen formula (individual physiology), not through different UI tracks.
